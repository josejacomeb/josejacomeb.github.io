I am highly motivated to apply to \textbf{Institution.} because of its prestigious environment at the intersection of Manufacturing and Automation,

As a Computer Vision engineer I could get meaningful experience in managing thousand of data of vehicles per day, automating in that way the integration between camera data and databases to provide better information to improve decisions about roads.

I have always been passionated about Robotics and Mechatronics, and throughout my academic journey I have combined both fields in practical and research-oriented projects. During my studies, I had the privilege of publishing two research papers related to these areas, which strengthened both my technical background and my motivation to contribute to advanced research in digital twins.

Morever, I have developed several personal and collaborative projects involving the integration of software and hardware systems, ranging from embedded systems to intelligent robotic applications. I invite you to check my GitHub portfolio for more information of my technical competences, reflecting both my initiative and continuous interest in innovation and autonomous systems.
